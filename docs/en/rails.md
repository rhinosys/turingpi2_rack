# L rails and rear locking — R3

The purchased rails are L sections marked **15.5 × 15.5 × 1.5 mm**. Their horizontal wing carries the load; printed saddles support both ends. The owner cuts the rails to fit after presenting the saddles, so no rack-depth assumption is built into the end supports.

## What changed after the physical test

The R2 lock was 30 mm from the rail end. The printed support did not line up with the metal hole. The user then reported hole centres at **15, 35 and 50 mm**. R3 selects **35 mm** and extends the rear shelf, ribs and socket by 5 mm while moving the M4 bore, nut pocket and underside access together.

Photos show **R2 before correction**, not a successful R3 fit. The ruler views are supporting evidence; the 35 mm target comes from the user's stated measurements. R3 has passed geometry and slicing checks; physical confirmation is pending.

## Mounting sequence

1. Place four saddles inside the rack: behind the front uprights and in front of the rear uprights. Use two M6 fixings per saddle, **eight total**.
2. Viewed from the rack front: AV-A = front left, AV-B = front right; AR-B = rear left, AR-A = rear right. Rotate rear saddles 180° around the vertical axis so their sockets face into the rack.
3. The M6 joint must clamp the PETG plate against the upright. A cage nut trapped between plate and upright prevents this; use a compatible nut position on the opposite face, with washer and sufficient screw length. Tighten the M6 connections before inserting the rails.
4. Present and cut the rails between the end stops, then deburr the cut. Insert the rear end until it reaches the stop, with the horizontal wing captured under the socket roof and the vertical wing clear.
5. Align the existing hole **35 mm from the rail end**. Fit the M4 nut into the top pocket; insert the M4 × 20 screw from underneath. It passes through the support, metal rail and socket roof. Do not force a misaligned screw or drill until the correct opening has been identified.
6. Tighten gently. The screw prevents withdrawal; it should not crush the PETG to close the socket gap. Check rail retention, chassis bearing contact and clearance above the lock before loading.

| M4 centre from rail end | 35 mm |

The socket roof rises about **6.6 mm above the rail's bearing surface**, plus any screw-tip projection. Position it outside the enclosure bearing area or provide the required clearance. No rack load rating has been established.
